The practice

Embed
Work inside the client's operation until the real process is visible, rather than the one written down.
Model
Turn what is there into an ontology and a knowledge graph: the entities the business actually runs on, joined, with every value recording where it came from.
Deploy
Put agents on top of that model, so answers are grounded in defined records rather than guessed from text.
Decide
Land on a decision the client acts on. The engagement is sold as that outcome, not as hours.

The modelling is the edge. Ontology and knowledge graph work is unusual for a solo engineer, and it is the layer Palantir sells as its Ontology product.

African mobility and logistics are evidence of what the method does, not the boundary of who it is for.
